# The Ultimate Pelona Hills Testimonial Request Email

**When to send:** 2–4 weeks after closing, once they've moved in but the excitement is still fresh.
**Personalize:** the two `[bracketed]` memory lines matter most. One real detail from *their* loan makes this feel like a personal note, not a campaign.

---

## Subject line options (pick one)

- `[First Name], how's the new house feeling?`
- `A small favor from your mortgage guy (totally optional)`
- `Your Pelona Hills story could help someone`
- `Quick question about your new home 🏡`

**Preview text:** *No script, no pressure. Just 60 seconds on your phone, if you're up for it.*

---

## The email

Hi [First Name],

It's been a few weeks since you got the keys, and I keep thinking about [personal detail, e.g. "the day you called me from the driveway" / "how excited the kids were to pick their rooms" / "your walkthrough at the model home"]. How's the new place treating you?

Thank you again for trusting me with your financing. I know buying a home is one of the biggest decisions you'll ever make, and I don't take it lightly that you let me be part of it. Helping families like yours get to closing day is honestly the best part of my job.

**I have a small favor to ask, and it's completely okay to say no.**

Would you be willing to record a short video about what your experience was *really* like?

Here's why. Almost every week I talk with someone looking at Pelona Hills who is quietly worried about something. Maybe it's the payment, the down payment, whether they'll qualify, or just not knowing how the process works. [Optional: "I remember you had some of those same questions at the start."] I can explain things all day, but hearing from a real family who has been through it helps them more than anything I could say.

Your honest story, in your own words, might be exactly what someone needs to hear.

**Here's how easy it is:**

- 📱 **Use your phone.** No fancy setup. Hold it sideways if you can.
- ⏱️ **Keep it short.** 60–120 seconds is perfect, and longer is fine if you're on a roll.
- 🏡 **Film at your new home if you'd like.** Out front, in the kitchen, on the back patio. Anywhere that feels like *you*.
- 🙌 **Don't prepare anything.** There's no script and no perfect answer. Laugh, pause, start over, bring the kids or the dog in. Real beats polished every time.

**Not sure what to say?** Just answer any of these, the way you'd tell a friend over dinner:

1. Before you found Pelona Hills, what was your home search like, and what were you hoping to find?
2. What was your biggest concern about buying the home or getting the financing?
3. Was there a moment you realized, "Okay, we can actually make this work"? What happened?
4. What did I do during the process that helped you the most?
5. Was anything about the mortgage process a good surprise?
6. How did it feel when you got the keys?
7. What would you tell another buyer who's thinking about Pelona Hills and working with me?

**Short on time?** Just answer these three:

- What were you worried about before buying?
- What made the biggest difference?
- What would you tell a friend thinking about buying here?

**Camera-shy? No problem.** Pick whatever's easiest:

- **Option A:** Record it yourself and text it to me at [phone], or upload it here: [link].
- **Option B:** Let's hop on a relaxed 10-minute video call. I'll ask the questions, you just talk, and I'll record it.
- **Option C:** If video isn't your thing, a few sentences in a reply to this email are just as appreciated.

**Your privacy matters.** I won't use anything publicly until I've sent you a short permission form showing exactly where and how it would appear, and you've signed it. You'll always have the final say, and I'll leave out your street address or anything personal you'd rather keep private.

Thank you for considering it, and even more for trusting me with your home. It's been a real privilege.

Welcome home,

John
[Title] | [Company]
[Phone] | [Email]
NMLS #[number]

**P.S.** Just say what actually happened. The small, specific details, like a worry you had or a phone call you remember, are what help other buyers the most.

## Compliance notes

- Get a **signed testimonial/media release** from everyone on camera before any public use.
- Have your company's **compliance team review** the final video. If a buyer mentions a rate, payment, or down payment, the video may need extra disclosures.
- If you send a thank-you gift, it must **not depend on the review being positive or on content**, and you may need to disclose it under FTC endorsement guidelines.
- Never edit clips in a way that changes the meaning of what the buyer said.
